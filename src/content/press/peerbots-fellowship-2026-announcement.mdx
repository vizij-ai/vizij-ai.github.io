Applications for the second [Peerbots](/organizations/peerbots/) Research Fellowship are now open.

The fellowship is a paid, 3-month, full-time program designed to support an early-career researcher — or researcher in training — advancing Human-Robot Interaction research. It supports a researcher working on **people-centered research of social robots**.

This year's fellowship runs in partnership with [Semio Community](/organizations/semio-community/), a nonprofit advancing the field of Human-Robot Interaction through reproducible software, hardware, and science. The [inaugural fellowship](/research/peerbots-fellowship-1/) was completed by [Dr. Dylan Thomas Doyle](/people/dylan-thomas-doyle/).

**The application deadline is May 22nd.** We encourage you to apply even if you're unsure you precisely match the description.

Learn more about the program and apply at the [Peerbots Research Fellowship page](https://www.peerbots.org/research-fellowship).
